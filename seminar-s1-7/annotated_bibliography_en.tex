\section{Core Reading (Foundational System Primitives)}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=6pt, topsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Anthropic. (Dec 2024).} \textit{Building Effective Agents.} Anthropic Research Whitepaper.\\
  \textbf{Focus Area:} Workflows vs. autonomous agents, foundational composition patterns (prompt chaining, routing, parallelization, orchestrator-workers, evaluator-optimizer), and the simplicity-first engineering thesis.\\
  \textcolor{critiquecolor}{\textbf{Critical Reflection:}} While the authors persuasively establish that predictable deterministic workflows vastly outperform autonomous agents for structured enterprise workloads, their simplicity-first thesis minimizes the severe engineering fragility of hard-coded routing thresholds and leaves state recovery for long-running workflows largely unaddressed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Extension Readings: Presenting Group Deep Dive}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em, itemsep=6pt, topsep=3pt, start=2]
  \item \textbf{Anthropic. (Feb 2025).} \textit{How We Built Our Multi-Agent Research System.} Anthropic Engineering Blog.\\
  \textbf{Focus Area:} Hierarchical Lead/Subagent decomposition, context window isolation, and architectural trade-offs in open-ended exploratory tasks.\\
  \textcolor{critiquecolor}{\textbf{Critical Reflection:}} Although partitioning exploration into isolated subagent contexts resolves attention dilution and context rot, it introduces an inevitable $3\times$--$8\times$ token inflation and a critical synthesis bottleneck where the Lead Agent frequently fails to detect subtle contradictory claims generated across independent subagent outputs.

  \item \textbf{OpenAI. (Feb 2025).} \textit{A Practical Guide to Building Agents.} OpenAI Developer Documentation.\\
  \textbf{Focus Area:} Model-directed tool calling loops, safety guardrails, structured evaluation harnesses, and progressive handoffs.\\
  \textcolor{critiquecolor}{\textbf{Critical Reflection:}} The guide promotes autonomous model-directed tool calling as the default target architecture yet glosses over how compounding failure rates ($P = p^n$) render unconstrained multi-turn loops unviable in high-stakes domains without deterministic schema validation and external non-LLM ground-truth oracles.

  \item \textbf{LangChain Team. (2024--2025).} \textit{LangGraph: Multi-Agent Workflows as Cyclic State Graphs.} Technical Documentation.\\
  \textbf{Focus Area:} Stateful multi-agent graphs, durable execution, persistence checkpointing, failure recovery, and human-in-the-loop interrupts.\\
  \textcolor{critiquecolor}{\textbf{Critical Reflection:}} While time-travel checkpointing and stateful persistence provide the indispensable durable execution missing from raw LLM APIs, forcing complex dynamic agent behaviors into rigid pre-compiled state graphs creates high maintenance friction and often degenerates into a cumbersome distributed finite-state machine.
\end{enumerate}
