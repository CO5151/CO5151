  \item \textbf{Deck architecture and division of labour:} the argument order (mechanism $\to$ limits $\to$ evidence $\to$ design), the mapping of the five mandatory items to slides, and both defence answers were decided by the group.
  \item \textbf{Fact-checking:} every figure, formula and citation was manually verified against the official abstract/PDF of the five readings before being placed on a slide.
\end{enumerate}
